Grid planner · every week to season close

Seven weeks. One repeating shape.

The shape never changes: kinda chic Monday, hero or announce Tuesday, What's On Wednesday, last week's photos Thursday, trend Friday, today-post Saturday, recap-as-invite Sunday. Only the assets rotate, and the week produces itself: the weekend photographer delivery feeds Thursday, Saturday's phone footage feeds Sunday, the recap sells the next weekend.

Three rules. Never reuse a photo on the grid within 14 days. Every post maps to a pillar (the yard, the people, what's on). Judge each slot on its own number: Thursday on saves, Wednesday on link taps, Sunday on watch-through. Stories run per the calendar doc.

Amber means rendered and ready today. Outlined means it needs the thing named on the chip: a line-up, a delivery, Saturday footage, or the shoot day.
