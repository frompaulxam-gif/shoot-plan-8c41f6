The three rules

  • Never miss the five. Mon to Fri grid, every week. Weekend grid stays off so there is never a slot you can't fill.
  • No photo twice on the grid within 14 days.
  • One story a day minimum, any pick from the reactive menu.

Playbook · grid

The five slots, with options

Monday · Recap montage

Cut from the videos that arrive after each weekend. 15 to 30 clips, one trending audio, cuts on the beat, under 20 seconds. End on the busiest wide. Caption sells the coming Friday, not the past: "Round two this Friday. Doors 5pm."

Fallback if a week's video is thin: photo-montage reel from the photographer set, same audio trick. Never skip the slot, downgrade it.

Tuesday · What's On carousel

Chelsea sends acts, offers and screenings. Slide 1 is always a photo with the weekend's headline over it, then one slide per thing, closing slide = doors times + book link. If nothing special is on: doors, DJ, drinks, food, done. Keep a saved template so this is 15 minutes.

Wednesday · Flexible, pick one menu

Drinks heroOne drink, one photo, one cheeky line. The orange garnish, the frozen pour, the glowing pint. Fastest post that exists.
Repost winnerRerun or remix the best performer of the last fortnight. Top carousel slide as a single, or the recap that got shares.
Photo singleOne standout photographer shot, minimal caption. The laugh, the hug, the overhead.
AnnounceAnything Chelsea flags midweek: an act, an offer, a screening. Takes priority over the menu when it exists.
Staff or vendorThe bartender pull, the till smile, the food truck. Faces build regulars.
ThrowbackOpening night, the first sunny Saturday, "three months of this". Works late-season.
Kinda chicOne-off: the rendered eight-slide carousel runs on ONE Wednesday, once. The rest of the set stays banked as emergency content.

Thursday · Last week's photos

6 to 8 from the weekly delivery. Lead with the best laugh. Deliberately include the mixed-age tables, they are the warmest shots and the crowd that books. Caption tags the night: "Saturday, you were great." Encourage tags, this is the saves-and-shares post.

Friday · Trend reel

You find and watch the shortlist. Bank anything adaptable in a saved folder (swipe file), then make the week's pick. When no live trend fits, pull from the queue:

  • The CCTV creature teaser (AI, stunt)
  • The brightness-slider reveal
  • Empty yard to full yard, same frame
  • The glasses-wipe drink reveal
  • Why people come / why people stay split screen
  • Drink · Vibes · Food three-beat loop
  • Blind pour challenge with staff
  • "You're invited btw" pinned-line montage

Spotting kit: burner account following the watchlist venues · their Highlights are the story archive · Meta Ad Library shows every story ad a venue runs · never log into third-party viewer sites.

For the venue team · Friday + Saturday

Live night story card

You run the account on trading nights. Six stories minimum across the night, in this order. Shoot vertical, sound on, tag @merchants.yard location.

1Doors. Friday 5pm / Saturday 2pm. A clip of the gate or the first pour + "doors open" text + location sticker.
2First drinks. Tap pour or a cocktail build, close up, 5 seconds.
3First tables in. The yard filling up. Never film anyone close-up without a nod.
4The act. DJ arriving, sound check, sax first lap. Name-tag them if they have an account.
5Peak. The busiest wide you can get, benches full, lights on. This is the money story.
6Last call / lights. "Same again tomorrow" (Fri) or "back Friday" (Sat).

Do

  • Vertical, steady, 5 to 10 seconds a clip
  • Sound on for music moments
  • Reshare anything guests tag us in
  • More often is fine, six is the floor

Don't

  • No flash, no filters, no stickers over faces
  • Nobody visibly drunk, ever
  • No close-ups without a quick yes
  • Don't delete flat stories, the run matters

Why it matters

Saturday's clips become Monday's recap post, and the recap sells next Friday. Six rough clips a night keep the whole week fed. Send anything good to Paul by Sunday evening.
