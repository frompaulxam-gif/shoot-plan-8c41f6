Carousel 2 · same nine photos, three openers

Pick the opener.

You were right that carousel 2 had drifted. Carousel 1 sells the place, this one sells a night out, so it gets its own format instead of borrowing the kinda chic opener and looking like a weaker copy. Same nine photos and the same running order in all three.

My pick is "you might". It is the only one short enough to keep the narrow three or four line stack the format lives on. The other two push the text block down the photo before the joke has started.
