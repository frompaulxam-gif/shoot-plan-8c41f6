Format from @lh_social_ · photos are your own

52 lines, two ways each. Pick your eight.

Every one is already laid over one of your own posted photos, pulled from the five carousels you sent, so what you are looking at is close to the finished slide rather than a list to imagine. Nothing here needs a new shoot.

Every photo now carries two options. The kinda chic version follows the original format. The brand voice version says the same thing the way the guidelines say to say it: like a good host, chatty, slightly cheeky, sentence case. Five of them use the guidelines' own wording and are marked.

The format, matched by measurement not by eye. Poppins Bold, which I identified from the original by its x-height to ascender ratio of 0.71 and its single-storey a. Type colour #fff1b4, a pale butter yellow rather than an off-white, sampled off all eight reference slides. 116px leading and a narrow measure that reproduces the original's own line breaks. Poppins is now installed on your Mac, so you can use it in Canva or CapCut too.

One rule worth holding to. Every line is something the reader does, never something the venue is. “kinda chic to have a hidden courtyard as your local” flatters them. “kinda chic that our cocktails are great” would just be smug, and it would cut against the unpretentious, social-not-exclusive line in the brand guidelines.
