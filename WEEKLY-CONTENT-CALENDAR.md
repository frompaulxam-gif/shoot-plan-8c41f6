# Merchants Yard: weekly content calendar

Built 10 Aug 2026. Venue trades Friday 5pm-midnight and Saturday 2pm-midnight, so the week is a build: afterglow Monday and Tuesday, planning moment Wednesday, proof Thursday, live Friday and Saturday, recap-as-invite Sunday.

Three pillars, per the Triple Down loop. Every slot maps to one:

1. **The Yard** (place and atmosphere) → tables, awareness
2. **The people** (recap, social proof) → engagement, tables
3. **What's on** (events, sport, offers) → tickets, tables

If a post serves none of these, it gets cut.

### Thursday poll bank
Polls about real choices outperform vibe checks and give you content back:
- "Sax or DJ this Saturday?"
- "Spritz or pint first?"
- "Friday or Saturday this week?"
- "Watching the match here or suffering at home?" (from 21 Aug)
- Question box: "what song gets the yard going?" Answers feed the DJ and next week's captions.

### Friday and Saturday live skeleton
Minimum five stories, in order: doors graphic → first pour (tap close-up) → first table in → act arriving or soundcheck → peak crowd → last call. The plate bank from the shoot plan covers quiet moments.

## The engine: each week produces itself

- Weekend stories feed Monday's UGC reshares.
- The photographer delivery feeds Thursday.
- Saturday's phone footage feeds Sunday's recap reel.
- The recap reel's pinned line makes it next week's advert.
- Kinda chic covers Monday for six weeks with zero new production.

One shoot feeds four to eight posts. Nothing in this calendar needs content that does not already exist or arrive weekly.

## Measuring it (per the Triple Down loop)

Judge each slot on its own metric, not on likes:
- Thursday photos: **saves and tags**
- Wednesday What's On: **link taps and DMs**
- Sunday recap: **watch-through and shares**
- Tuesday announce: **DMs and table enquiries**

Review after four weeks. Kill whichever slot is bottom on its own metric and give the space to whichever is top. Note: the grid currently gets about 3 likes a post, so the first month's numbers are the baseline, not the verdict.

## If capacity gets tight

Priority order: **Thursday > Sunday > Wednesday > Friday > Monday > Tuesday > Saturday.** The last three drop first.
