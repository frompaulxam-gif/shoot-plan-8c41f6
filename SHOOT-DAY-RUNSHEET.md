# Merchants Yard - shoot day run sheet

One Saturday. 17 of 19 formats. Print this or keep it open on your phone.

## Before you leave
- [ ] Charged: camera, 2 spare batteries, phone, ND filter
- [ ] Tripod + gaffer tape (for the marks) + clamp for the overhead rig
- [ ] Second phone (it is a prop in two of the shots)
- [ ] A physical menu (prop for the page-turn shot)
- [ ] A pair of glasses, plus someone willing to wear them (the wipe shot)
- [ ] Venue confirmed: overhead mount allowed, filming notice at the door, bar staff briefed for 13:00

---

## 10:45 - Load in
Find where the sun lands at midday. Pick the hero table.
**Tape three tripod marks now**, one per empty-to-full pair:
- [ ] 01 Overhead wide of the whole yard
- [ ] 02 The bar
- [ ] 03 The bench tables

**Solve the overhead height before anything else.** Upstairs window, balcony, or the top of a ladder. If there is no height in the yard, the pair 01 idea changes and you need to know that now, not at 21:00.

Photo each tape position. Write down the focal length and exposure.

## 11:00 - Block A, clean drinks (best light)
Shoot 60fps.
- [ ] POV seated, hands in frame, drink lifted to camera
- [ ] Straw stir, slow
- [ ] Clink, two glasses
- [ ] Top-down with the logo on the table or glass
- [ ] Condensation macro
- [ ] Wide of the table with the yard behind
- [ ] Stills for the grid carousel, same setup

## 12:15 - Block A, gimmicks (CAMERA DOES NOT MOVE)
- [ ] Empty table pass, hold 5s
- [ ] Same frame, drink already there, hold 5s  *(appear-cut, two shots)*
- [ ] Phone flat on table showing a photo of the drink, finger taps it
- [ ] Hand turning menu pages, one pass per item
- [ ] Flat/dark pass then bright pass, identical frame  *(brightness slider)*
- [ ] Glasses wipe: blurred POV, glasses off and cleaned, frame snaps sharp on the drink

## 13:00 - Block F, staff
- [ ] Blind Pour Challenge, 4-5 staff, let them fail
- [ ] Reactions, not just the pour
- [ ] 2-3 extra challenge ideas while everyone is there

## 13:40 - Block E, plate bank (15 min, no people, no movement)
15 seconds each, locked off:
- [ ] Entrance from as high as you can get
- [ ] Bar
- [ ] Big screen
- [ ] Stage / DJ position
- [ ] Full yard wide
- [ ] Seating from the corner
- [ ] Ask for the real CCTV angle or a still from it

## 13:55 - Block B, three empty passes
Same lens, same height, same exposure on each. Nobody in frame.
- [ ] 01 Overhead wide, nobody in the yard
- [ ] 02 The bar, clean and set, nobody being served
- [ ] 03 The bench tables, empty and laid out

## 14:00 - DOORS
- [ ] Mount the overhead rig above the entrance, start it rolling, leave it

## 14:00 to 21:00 - Block C, the room
**Shoot in threes: one drink macro, one room wide, one people moment.** Ten times through the night.
Frame wide enough to crop 4:3 and 9:16 from the same take.
- [ ] 14:00-17:00 first groups arriving, drinks going out
- [ ] 17:00-19:00 golden hour, faces, groups mixing, low sun through glasses
- [ ] 19:00-21:00 peak, DJ or act, full yard, the screen, bar working
- [ ] Cast on purpose for the after-work and 30-45 crowd, not just the youngest table

## Block G, people (17:00 onwards)
Face level, handheld, **inside** the group. Get a proper yes from anyone who carries a shot.
- [ ] **17:00 one person, one drink.** Staff carry a drink out, hand it over, say the line. After-work crowd, not the youngest table.
- [ ] **19:00+ inside the crowd.** Be in position BEFORE the goal or the drop, not after it.

Bank these five looks through the night:
- [ ] Staff mid-task, looking up
- [ ] Someone getting their photo taken
- [ ] A group under the signage
- [ ] Backs of the bar queue
- [ ] The full wide with the string lights on

## 21:00 to 23:00 - Block B, three full passes
Back on the same three marks. The payoff.
- [ ] 01 Same overhead wide, yard busy
- [ ] 02 Same bar, three deep and working
- [ ] 03 Same tables, **people dancing**. Shoot this one last, it may not be there until closer to 23:00

## 23:00 - Wrap
- [ ] Pull the overhead rig
- [ ] Card offload before you leave the car
- [ ] Note which formats you actually got, in the plan file

---

## Do not forget
1. Tripod locked for all of block A. Every appear-cut dies if it moves.
2. Two exposures, identical frame, for the brightness reel. Not a grade fix.
3. Split screens and the CCTV reel are **4:3**. Everything else 9:16.
4. The plate bank is the highest-value 15 minutes of the day. It funds every AI post to Christmas.
5. **Three empty-to-full pairs, three taped marks.** Overhead needs height, dancing needs patience.
