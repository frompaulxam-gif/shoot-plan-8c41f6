## Two things worth knowing before posting

**1. Trial reels go to non-followers first.** The caption has to land for someone who has never heard of the place. That means Leicester gets named, the days get named, and nothing relies on being a regular. It also means keep it short: cold audiences do not read past line one.

**2. The concept fights the format, on purpose.** "Life without trending audio" only works if the audio is genuinely the venue: the live act, the crowd, the DJ in the room. That is the whole flex. But it does give up one of the reach levers a trial reel normally uses, so the hook and the watch-through have to carry it. Worth knowing rather than being surprised by the numbers.

**3. You have three hooks, not four.** `est1.mp4` and `test4.mp4` have **byte-identical first two seconds** (same decoded checksum), so they are the same hook with est1 running 1.8s longer. Do not run them as two separate trials, you would be testing nothing.

| File | Opening shot | Best on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| **est1.mp4** (21.4s) | Food truck, group toasting at the bench, daylight | Life without phones |
| **test4.mp4** (19.6s) | Same opening, tighter cut | Life without phones |
| **take2_different start.mp4** | LED wall, close dancing, night colour | Life without phones |
| **take2 different start 2.mp4** | Wide, the whole yard packed | Life without trending audio |

My read: **"Life without phones" belongs on the dancing** (nobody in that shot is holding a screen, which is the proof), and **"Life without trending audio" belongs on the wide** where you can see a whole room moving to something.

## How I would run the trials

Three hooks, so three trials, one at a time so you learn something:

1. **Start with the wide** (take2 different start 2) plus caption A. Biggest, clearest, easiest for a stranger to understand in one second.
2. **Then the dancing** (take2 different start) plus caption A. This is the one most likely to hold watch-through.
3. **Then the food truck** (test4, the tighter cut, not est1). Slowest opening of the three, so it is the control.

Whichever wins on watch-through, post that one properly to followers. Do not run est1 as well, it is the same hook.

**Every one of them:** tag the location as Merchants Yard, sound on, and keep the caption to the first two lines visible before the "more" cut.

## One correction I owe you

I earlier told you the grid was getting **about 3 likes a post** and I put that in the calendar doc as the baseline. The profile shows **11,846 followers**, which makes 3 likes implausible. The number came from Instagram's API returning `like_count: 3` identically on all five carousels, which almost certainly means **like counts are hidden on that account**, not that engagement is dead.

So ignore that line in the calendar doc. Pull the real numbers from the account's own insights before judging any slot, and treat saves, shares and watch-through as the measures rather than likes, which was the right advice for a different reason.
